About

Engineering is the work.
Judgment is the discipline.

I am the founder of Emilo Labs, where I design and build software across identity, privacy, security and applied AI.

I work across the stack, but my focus is on the decisions that shape a system before and after the code is written: trust boundaries, data flows, threat models, failure modes and the trade-offs that need to be made explicit.

I have built products independently, conducted user research before implementation and carried systems through internal security review. I also write about privacy, data sovereignty and the social consequences of technical design.
